## Why GitCoin is Perfect for RealMint

### 1. **Community-First Approach**

GitCoin Grants rewards projects that resonate with the community:
- ✅ Public goods (identity = everyone benefits)
- ✅ Open source (MIT licensed)
- ✅ Community voting (transparent allocation)
- ✅ Matching funds (quadratic funding amplifies impact)

**RealMint fits perfectly:** We're building something the community votes on, not venture-funded project.

### 2. **Faster Funding Cycles**

- GitCoin rounds happen every quarter
- Each round: 2-week voting period
- Fast turnaround for small-to-medium projects

**RealMint advantage:** We can get $10-50K per round vs. 3-month wait for ESP

### 3. **Lower Barrier to Entry**

- No lengthy application process
- No VC connections required
- Just show up with good project + community

**RealMint advantage:** Team is developer-first, not fundraiser-first

## Project Profile

### What We're Building

**Public Infrastructure:**
- Decentralized identity registry (on-chain)
- Attestation system (EIP-712 signing)
- Credit scoring oracle (privacy-preserving)
- Under-collateralized lending (25-50% collateral)

**Real-World Integration:**
- Plaid for bank data (CeFi ↔ DeFi bridge)

- Production deployment (Vercel + Render)

```

RealMint Solution:
```

## FAQ for GitCoin Community

**Q: Is this a pump-and-dump?**
A: No. We have no token and no trading. This is pure infrastructure (like OpenZeppelin or Gnosis).

**Q: Why do you need funding? Aren't you making money off lending?**
A: Not yet—we haven't launched. This grant funds the infrastructure (smart contracts, security, production setup) before any revenue model.

**Q: How is this different from Compound/Aave?**
A: They're lending protocols. We're the **identity layer** that other lending protocols can use. Think Uniswap (DEX) vs. USDC (stablecoin).

**Q: Can I use this with my hardware wallet?**
A: Yes! We support EIP-6963 wallet detection, which includes Ledger, Trezor, etc.

**Q: What's your plan for regulation?**
A: We're building a compliance framework from day 1 (audit trail, off-chain PII, etc.). Legal review scheduled before launch.
