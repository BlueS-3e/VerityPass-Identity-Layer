### Grant Project Innovation
**What is unique and innovative about your grant project?**

1. **Attestation-as-primitive**: Unlike competitors (Aave, Compound), we position financial attestations as core DeFi infrastructure rather than risk layers. Enables new use cases: insurance underwriting, employment-based lending, supply chain finance.

2. **EIP-712 Signature Verification**: Implements gasless metratransactions via EIP-712, reducing user friction and cost barriers in emerging markets. First on BNB Chain.

3. **Dynamic Oracle-Driven Fees**: Real-time price feeds automatically adjust platform fees based on market conditions. Ensures sustainability without governance overhead.

4. **Referral Incentive Model**: Built-in rebate system bootstraps adoption through community growth. Referrers earn yield on subsequent transactions, creating long-term engagement loops.

5. **Risk-Aware Liquidity Pools**: Combines attestation scores with Aave-style lending pools, enabling fair pricing for emerging market borrowers (vs. blanket 20%+ rates from traditional lenders).
